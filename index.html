📊 Sample Size Calculator for Bland–Altman Limits of Agreement

Free online tool for clinical research and method comparison studies

This application provides a scientifically robust method for calculating the required sample size in agreement studies using the Bland–Altman limits of agreement approach.

Users can input study-specific parameters, including significance level, expected standard deviation, allowable margin of error, and anticipated dropout rate. The calculator then generates the optimal sample size required to obtain reliable and precise agreement estimates.
